❓ HOW TO PLAY

Objective

Sort the colors so each tube contains 4 identical colors and the two empty tubes remain empty.

Moving colors

Tap a tube to select the bottom contiguous block of identical colors. Tap another tube to move the block there. You can only move to an empty tube or onto a matching bottom color.

Customizing colors

Click the 🎨 COLORS button to open the color editor. Use the color pickers to change any of the 10 game colors. Your custom palette is saved automatically.

Show Letters

Toggle the 🔤 SHOW LETTERS button to display a letter on each ball for better color distinction.

Undo / Restart

Use UNDO to revert moves one by one. RESTART resets the current puzzle.
